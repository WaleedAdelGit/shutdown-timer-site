Effective Date: 14 September 2026

Contact: waleedproj1@gmail.com

1. Core Timer and Accessibility Service

The app does not require users to create an account. Timer duration and a small amount of application state are stored locally on the user's device.

Safe Shutdown Timer includes an Android Accessibility Service for one specific purpose: after a timer selected by the user expires, the service requests Android to display the system power menu.

The Accessibility Service is configured so that it cannot retrieve window content. The app does not use Accessibility to read screen content, type text, perform gestures, click the Power Off confirmation, or collect or share Accessibility data.

Enabling the Accessibility helper is optional. A prominent disclosure is shown before the user is taken to Android Accessibility settings.

2. Advertising and Consent

The free version of Safe Shutdown Timer uses Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP).

Depending on the user's region, consent choices, device configuration, and Google's advertising settings, Google advertising technologies may process information such as IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, advertising identifiers, device information, and related data for purposes including advertising, analytics, consent management, security, and fraud prevention.

Where required, the app requests consent information through Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting advertising.

For additional information about how Google processes data, please review Google's privacy policies and advertising documentation.

3. Remove Ads Purchase

The optional Remove Ads upgrade is a one-time purchase processed through Google Play Billing.

The app may receive information about the status and entitlement of the purchase so that it can determine whether advertising should be disabled.

Safe Shutdown Timer does not receive the user's full payment card number or bank account details. Payment processing is handled by Google Play.

4. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell Accessibility Service data, timer contents, or locally stored timer information.

Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and its service providers according to Google's terms, privacy policies, and the user's consent choices.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Timer settings and local application state remain on the user's device until the user clears the application's data or uninstalls the app.

Network communications performed by Google SDKs use the security mechanisms provided by those services.

6. User Choices

Users can:

  • Disable the Accessibility helper at any time in Android Settings.
  • Cancel an active shutdown timer.
  • Use Google's advertising privacy options when available.
  • Purchase the one-time Remove Ads upgrade to disable advertising.
  • Clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

7. Children's Privacy

Safe Shutdown Timer is not specifically designed for children. Advertising and consent behavior is handled according to the settings configured for the app and applicable Google Play and Google advertising requirements.

This Privacy Policy may be updated if application functionality, third-party SDKs, Google Play requirements, or legal requirements change. The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
