Privacy Policy

Shutdown Timer (No Root)

Package: com.safeshutdowntimer.powerdialog
Effective date: September 11, 2026

This policy explains how Shutdown Timer (No Root) handles information when you use the app. The app does not require you to create an account with the developer.

1. Information handled by the app

The core timer functionality stores limited settings locally on your device, such as timer state, language preference, app preferences, and a local cache of whether ads have been removed. This local information is not sent to a developer-operated server.

2. Advertising

The app may display ads using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from network information, app interactions, diagnostics, and ad performance information, subject to your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google's policies.

Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request or manage advertising consent before personalized advertising is used.

3. One-time purchase to remove ads

The optional “Remove Ads” purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details. Google Play may provide the app with purchase status and product entitlement information so the app can unlock the ad-free experience and restore eligible purchases.

4. Accessibility Service

The app uses an Android Accessibility Service only when you explicitly enable it. Its limited purpose is to request Android's system power dialog when a timer you created reaches zero.

The app is designed not to read, collect, transmit, store, or sell the contents of your screen through the Accessibility Service, and it does not use Accessibility data for advertising or profiling. The final shutdown action remains controlled by Android's system interface and the user.

5. Alarms and notifications

The app may use Android alarm and notification features to trigger the timer at the requested time and show timer status. These features are used for app functionality and are not used by the developer to collect personal information.

6. Data sharing

The developer does not operate a backend that sells your personal information. Third-party services integrated into the app, including Google Mobile Ads, UMP, and Google Play Billing, may process data as described in their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored app settings remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Purchase records are managed by Google Play under your Google account. Advertising-related information processed by Google is handled according to Google's retention and privacy controls.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to limit the app to the permissions and platform APIs needed for its stated functions. No method of software distribution or storage can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly request children to provide personal information directly to the developer. Distribution and advertising settings should be configured in Google Play Console and AdMob according to the target audience selected for the app.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

11. Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact: waleedproj1@gmail.com
