# Privacy Policy — Safe Shutdown Timer

Effective Date: 14 September 2026

Safe Shutdown Timer is provided by Waleed Adel. Contact:waleedproj1@gmail.com].

## Core timer and Accessibility

The app does not require an account. Timer duration and a small amount of app state are stored locally on the device.

The app includes an Android AccessibilityService for one limited purpose: after a timer selected by the user expires, the service requests Android to display the system power menu. The service is configured so it cannot retrieve window content. The app does not use Accessibility to read screen content, type text, perform gestures, click the Power off confirmation, or collect/share Accessibility data. Enabling the helper is optional, and a prominent disclosure is shown before the user is taken to Android Accessibility settings.

## Advertising and consent

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Depending on the user's region, choices and Google's configuration, Google's advertising technologies may process information such as IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device/account identifiers for advertising, analytics, consent management and fraud prevention.

The app requests consent information through UMP before requesting ads and exposes Google privacy options when required.

Google's privacy information is available through Google's own policies and Mobile Ads documentation.

## Purchases

The optional `remove_ads` upgrade is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play Billing. The app receives purchase status/entitlement information needed to unlock the ad-free experience, but it does not receive the user's full card or bank account details.

## Data sharing

The developer does not sell Accessibility data or timer contents. Advertising-related data may be processed/shared by Google as described above and according to the user's consent choices and Google's terms.

## Security and retention

Local app state remains on the device unless the app is removed or its data is cleared. Network communications made by Google SDKs use their supported secure transport. Android backups are disabled for this app.

## Choices

Users can:

- disable the Accessibility helper at any time in Android settings;
- cancel an active timer;
- use Google's ad privacy options when shown/required;
- buy the one-time Remove Ads product to disable banner and app-open ads;
- clear app data or uninstall the app.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when app features, SDKs or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when that happens.
